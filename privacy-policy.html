ClickMint Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. ClickMint protects your personal information and uses only the data required to provide rewards, wallet, referrals and app security features.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect basic account information such as your name, mobile number, referral code, wallet activity, completed tasks, device information and app usage activity.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to create your account, track tasks, calculate rewards, manage wallet balance, process withdrawals, improve user experience and maintain app security.

3. Wallet & Withdrawal Data

For withdrawal requests, we may collect payout details such as UPI ID, requested amount, transaction status and request history.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Services

ClickMint may display ads through third-party providers such as Google AdMob. These services may collect advertising identifiers and usage information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Limited information may be shared only with trusted service providers for app functionality, analytics, advertising or fraud prevention.

6. Security

We use reasonable security measures to protect user information. However, no online platform is completely secure.

7. Fraud Prevention

We may monitor activity to detect fake accounts, suspicious rewards abuse or automated usage.

8. User Rights

Users may request correction or deletion of personal data, subject to security and legal requirements.

9. Children’s Privacy

ClickMint is intended for users who are legally allowed to use reward-based applications in their region.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
